Our Story

Named after Florentine.

In 1997, a man planted vines on a hill above the village of Majdel Maouche, in the heart of the Chouf, and gave the land a name: Florentine, the woman he loved. Every row, every barrel, every bottle began as a gift to her.

He brought grape varieties from France and Spain and let them take root in Lebanese soil. In the château's cellar, the wines rest patiently in oak barrels and stainless steel vats, because great wines aren't rushed, they're perfected by time.

From that hill came Clos d'Hébé, Le Bouquet, the Grand Vin and Dame Noire. Today their son works beside his father, carrying forward what his parents began, one harvest at a time.

In loving memory of Florentine, whose name this château carries and whose spirit lives in every bottle.
